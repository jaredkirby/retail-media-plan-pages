01 / Direction

Five shared challenges. One first proof.

The team framework remains the larger map. Start where current evidence can improve execution control and follow-through. This pilot links those two challenges to planning authority, trustworthy evidence, and repeatable work.

03Understand resultsShow what the evidence proves and what remains unknown.
04Decide & resolveFirst focus: give each exception an owner and verify closure.
05Repeat & improveRetain evidence, decisions, and tests so another person can continue.

Build now

A daily exception process

Compare current plans, scoped operating instructions, saved controls, and qualified delivery. Produce a short review with the conflict, evidence, owner, deadline, and next action.

Defer

The general platform

Pause app expansion, multi-user infrastructure, unattended campaign actions, and broad learning automation. Revisit them when this pilot demonstrates the need.

Success is a correct decision carried to verified closure. A completed refresh, a generated report, or a short alert list does not establish account safety.

03 / Three kinds of evidence

Funding, permission, and delivery are separate.

Financial plan

Shopperations

Current planning identities, allocations, dates, statuses, and cost basis. Retain revisions and the accepted meaning of each financial field.

Operating permission

Accepted instructions

HOLD, STOP, build, launch, release, and intentional overrides. Record their exact scope, prohibited activity, effective interval, evidence, and superseding decisions.

Observed execution

Skai

Native campaigns, dated cost, control settings, shared membership, and source limits. Admit Portfolio funding, carryover, cycles, and synchronization evidence separately.

A Committed budget does not authorize launch. A budget-setup override does not automatically authorize delivery. A platform cap does not become the financial commitment.

Use the existing accepted ingestion path for each evidence purpose. MCP access or a successful metadata fetch cannot promote an observation into qualified pacing evidence.

Stable, scoped identityKeep planning IDs distinct from native Skai IDs. Include client, retailer, profile, campaign, and approved shared-control membership.
Separate clocksKeep source update time, retrieval time, reporting period, decision time, and instruction effective time distinct.
Complete selected scopeProve required profiles, campaign inventory, dates, filters, and pages. Include campaigns outside packet mappings.
Current source healthRecompute freshness for the decision. Separate reporting delay from retrieval age and synchronization uncertainty.

04 / The operating loop

Detect. Assign. Investigate. Verify.

Run a staffed, read-only process. A model can explain supported findings and prepare investigation. Deterministic checks own detection, calculation, and authority boundaries.

01

Retain evidence

Pin source, instruction, mapping, and rule revisions. Evaluate current freshness and coverage.

02

Open findings

Check planned controls against delivery, then spending campaigns against planning and instruction authority.

03

Route a decision

Assign an owner and next check. A human decides any containment or correction through the existing authorized process.

04

Verify closure

Retain the response and qualifying follow-up evidence. Reopen the finding when relevant facts change.

Evidence-dependent review

Keep the strict gate

Financial assessment and clearance require current, qualified evidence. Preserve stale-review rejection, scope checks, supersession, and separate campaign-change permission.

A blocked or failed evaluation cannot clear the account.

New bounded remediation

Investigate blocked findings

Allow owner assignment, evidence requests, and read-only notes when inputs are missing, canceled, stale, or failed. Bind them to the current finding revision, retained observation, and latest relevant attempt.

Completing an investigation cannot accept an amendment, restore financial eligibility, or authorize a campaign action.

A HOLD incident can be supported by verified cost and an effective instruction even when budget authority is unresolved. Assess the incident’s own required evidence; do not mark the financial scope healthy.

Every exception carries the next decision.

The conflictObserved values, the applicable instruction or plan, and the delivery dates involved.
The evidenceSource snapshots, timestamps, native identities, exact membership, rule revision, and coverage limits.
The owner and responseA named operator, next action, deadline, required authority, and next check.
The closure testThe later evidence that would establish resolution. Retain unresolved obligations and source gaps.

Check the conflict without inventing authority.

06 / Build sequence

Deliver small slices with explicit exits.

Name the implementation owner before coding. Keep each slice bounded, preserve unrelated work, and verify it against the existing reconciliation tests plus the new failure cases.

  1. Fix the pilot’s evidence contract

    Declare selected Walmart profiles and expected inventory. Specify planning identity, shared membership, cost basis, reporting clocks, qualified control observations, and instruction precedence. Record source gaps rather than widening authority.

    ExitA dated, reproducible decision bundle; unsupported fields and missing coverage remain explicit.
    Jared confirms pilot scope and accepted instructions. The implementation owner documents admission rules.

  2. Add the missing detection paths

    Implement the reverse campaign scan and rules for effective HOLD/STOP, cancellations, flight boundaries, and changed budgets. Keep incidents independently assessable when financial evidence is blocked.

    ExitPositive, negative, and uncertain cases produce the expected finding type without shared-budget double counting.
    The implementation owner adds rules and fixtures. An independent reviewer checks source and authority semantics.

  3. Add remediation in the same ledger

    Permit evidence-bound assignment and investigation of blocked findings. Include instruction revisions in reopening behavior. Preserve transactions, leases, failed-run visibility, evidence hashes, supersession, and safe retry identity.

    ExitBlocked findings receive owners; obsolete work and unsupported financial clearance remain rejected. Interruption preserves coherent state.
    The implementation owner extends bounded transitions. The reviewer checks failure and recovery cases.

  4. Replay retained October evidence

    Test observed planning changes, the scoped cancellation decisions, the budget-setup override, and unresolved synchronization. Add a separate positive HOLD case only when its instruction and delivery interval are established.

    ExitA reviewed expected-results set with no fabricated incident. Known missing facts stay unresolved.
    Jared confirms the business interpretation. The reviewer separates retained history from synthetic test cases.

  5. Run staffed shadow reviews

    Evaluate current qualified sources, review exceptions, and follow at least one real finding through verified closure. Retain false alerts, missed issues, source failures, review effort, and response delays.

    ExitThe five acceptance gates pass on actual work. Jared decides whether the demonstrated benefit warrants expansion.
    Jared leads the trial. Each finding has a named response owner and next check.

07 / Acceptance

Earn expansion through operating evidence.

  1. Source coverage

    Complete selected-profile inventory and dated costs; qualified planning evidence; exact shared membership; separately admitted Portfolio/control observations. The first claim remains AFNA Walmart.

  2. Instruction fidelity

    Preserve scoped overrides and cancellations. Detect a supported HOLD case and avoid false violations from legitimate pre-cancellation history. Unknown or mid-day boundaries retain their limits.

  3. Detection correctness

    Catch unmapped spend and changed budgets; bypass pacing materiality for HOLD/STOP; classify uncertainty; prevent duplicate spend and funding totals.

  4. Review and remediation reliability

    Unchanged evidence avoids duplicate investigations. Relevant instruction or delivery changes reopen work. Blocked findings can be investigated without clearance. Recovery and retry preserve history. One real exception reaches verified closure.

  5. Operating usefulness

    Measure source/reporting lag, detection delay, false and missed alerts, review minutes, response time, closure time, and repeat investigation effort.

< 15 minTarget for Jared’s review of the actual exception packet. A fast review of incomplete evidence fails.
10 daysProposed staffed business-day trial, not a validated promotion threshold. Record incomplete-data days as failures.
1 real closureMinimum operating proof beyond alert generation. Retain the decision, response, and qualifying verification.

Expand when coverage, correctness, response, and reliability are demonstrated. Elapsed trial days alone are insufficient. A read-only guardian detects exposure; a separately authorized human response prevents further spend. Do not count an alert as prevented dollars.

Continue when

The loop is useful

Exceptions are accurate, timely, owned, and verifiable. Review fits the target without hiding critical findings. Evidence shows less repeated checking and a credible response path.

Repair before expanding when

The proof fails

Coverage is partial, source delay defeats response, instruction scope is uncertain, false alerts consume review, or findings stall. Preserve valid independent work; resolve the failed gate.

08 / Basis and limits

Keep the evidence honest.

  • The existing audits support reuse.The MacBook Pro review reported 99 reconciliation tests and a passing repository health check. These establish bounded code behavior, not live account safety or time saved.
  • The October record supports change and cancellation replay.The retained instructions establish a scoped budget-setup override and cancellation of three removed October rows. They do not establish blanket delivery permission.
  • A positive HOLD-spend replay remains unestablished.It needs the exact applicable instruction and complete delivery facts in the prohibited interval. Earlier cost on a separately paused campaign is not that proof. Preliminary budget comparisons also retained synchronization uncertainty.
  • Healthy acquisition does not clear execution.The retained October 9 Shopperations refresh had search and onsite eligibility, with offsite publication still blocked. That is source-health evidence, not campaign-execution clearance.
